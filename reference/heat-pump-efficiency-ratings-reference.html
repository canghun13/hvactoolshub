Efficiency metric lookup

Matched-condition conversion

EER (BTU/Wh) = COP × 3.412141633
COP = EER ÷ 3.412141633

This conversion is valid only when COP and EER describe the same cooling output and electrical input boundary at the same condition. It does not convert a seasonal rating to a point COP, or predict annual cost.

How to identify the right value

  1. State whether the question is heating or cooling and whether it concerns a moment, a measured interval, or a whole season.
  2. Keep load, equipment data, and runtime at the same operating condition.
  3. Use COP for the operating-cost calculator only when it comes from a matching condition.
  4. Keep SEER2 and HSPF2 as their certified seasonal ratings unless an official model provides the appropriate conversion method.

Example

A cooling COP of 3.00 at a given test point corresponds to EER 10.24 BTU/Wh at that same point (3.00 × 3.412141633). It does not mean the unit has a SEER2 of 10.24, because SEER2 is a seasonal test metric.

Limits and next steps

Efficiency labels do not establish whether the equipment has enough capacity at a design condition, whether an electrical circuit is adequate, or how a particular building will operate. Verify the system's certified combination and manufacturer performance data, then use an appropriate load calculation and qualified design process.
